# Asset provenance — City Illustration V2

Created 2026-09-05 for HumanOverrideOverloadUE by Codex using local Blender 4.5.12.

All seven meshes, their three authored LODs, facade lettering and the color/ORM atlas were authored by the project scripts from mathematical geometry and seeded texture synthesis. No third-party mesh, image, stock library or generative 3D service was downloaded or used. The project title image was viewed as an art-direction reference; it was not projected onto 3D geometry or included in these textures.

Editable source: CityIllustration_Source.blend. The original generator is archived outside the active project; edit the retained Blender master for future tree changes. manifest.json records FBX, texture and Blender source SHA256 hashes. Derived Unreal assets are under /Game/SkylineRush/Environment. Final distribution and ownership review remains with the project owner.
